Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy describes the current website and app. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, uses Google Fonts, and sends customers to Stripe for checkout. Their handling of information is also governed by their own policies.

1. Information We Handle

Website visits: GitHub Pages serves this static website. Hosting providers and network operators may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser, and requested page, to deliver and protect the service. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser may connect to Google to retrieve those font files.

Purchases: When you follow the checkout link, Stripe processes information you provide, such as contact, payment, and transaction details. We do not receive or store your full card number through this static website. Stripe's own privacy notice explains its processing and any services or payment features it operates.

App data: The current app stores appearance, onboarding, guide-progress, usage-time, and tweak-selection data locally on your Mac. It also stores prior macOS preference values needed to attempt to restore supported changes. The app applies selected changes through macOS preference settings. The current app does not include analytics, advertising, or telemetry code that sends this local app data to us.

2. Cookies And Similar Storage

The website code does not set its own analytics or advertising cookies. Stripe or other third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies on their own pages. The app uses local browser storage inside the app for local settings and progress; this data remains on your device unless you or another program exports it.

3. Why Information Is Processed

Technical information is used by hosting and network providers to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the website. Stripe processes purchase information to handle checkout, payment, receipts, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Local app information supports the app's settings, onboarding, and restoration features.

4. Service Providers And Overseas Processing

GitHub hosts this website and release files, Google supplies remotely loaded fonts, and Stripe provides checkout and payment services. These providers may process information in locations outside your country under their own terms and privacy policies. Review their policies before using their services.

5. Retention And Security

Website hosting and payment providers determine retention periods for their service records under their policies and legal obligations. App settings and progress remain in the app's local data on your Mac until you remove them or uninstall the app and its data. No internet service or storage method is completely secure; protect your device and backups.

6. Your Choices And Requests

You can remove local app data by deleting Krypton's local application data from your Mac, understanding that this also removes saved selections and restoration information. For questions or privacy requests about a purchase, use the contact details on your Stripe receipt. For information handled directly by GitHub, Google, or Stripe, contact that provider as described in its privacy policy.

7. Children

Krypton is general Mac utility software and is not designed to collect information from children. Do not submit another person's personal information through checkout or support without authority to do so.

8. Changes And Contact

We may update this policy by publishing a new version here and changing the “Last updated” date. For privacy questions about a purchase, use the contact details provided with your Stripe receipt. Avoid sending sensitive personal information through public issue trackers.
